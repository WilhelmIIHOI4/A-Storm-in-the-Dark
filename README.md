# A Storm in the Dark — HOI IV Mod

This modification offers a unique opportunity to visualize the SS-Ordensstaat Gotenland as it might have existed in 1936. It is acknowledged that this scenario is not grounded in historical accuracy, as the modification's primary objective is not to replicate or simulate real-world historical events. Instead, it explores an alternate historical trajectory.

Within this mod, players will encounter the emergence of several new political entities. These include, but are not limited to, the Dark Order of Gotenland, the Republic of Ukraine, and the Kuban People's Republic. Additionally, various other nations are posited to appear within the Caucasian region, contributing to a significantly altered geopolitical landscape.

At the current stage of development, the Dark Order of Gotenland is the most comprehensively realized of these new nations, featuring a detailed implementation. For the other listed emerging states, specifically the Republic of Ukraine, the Kuban People's Republic, and the aforementioned Caucasian entities, the development has extended to the creation of distinct military forces. However, beyond these military assets, further development for these particular nations has not yet been undertaken.

Future updates are planned to systematically expand upon the existing content. This expansion will involve the introduction of additional details, fleshing out the features and attributes of the newly introduced nations, and potentially refining existing mechanics. The intention is to gradually build out the world and its interactive elements, thereby enhancing the player's experience and the overall depth of the simulation. This iterative development process aims to progressively enrich the mod with further content and complexities, ensuring a more complete and engaging experience over time.
## Installation

To implement this modification, users are required to access the game's launcher. Within the launcher, a new mod profile should be created, allowing for any designated name. Subsequently, the downloaded files pertaining to the mod must be transferred into the directory of this newly created mod profile.
